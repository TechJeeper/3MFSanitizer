Universal 3MF Sanitizer

Convert proprietary 3MFs (Bambu/Prusa) to clean 3MFs for any slicer

How it works: Bambu Studio 3MFs use a production-extension layout that Cura, PrusaSlicer, and Anycubic cannot import. This tool flattens that into a standard 3MF, keeps painted colors and filament colors, and carries over designer print settings (walls, layer width, infill, supports) so MakerWorld profiles still slice the way they were tuned.
